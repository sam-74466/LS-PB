1. About the service

Leakage Specialist provides thermal imaging inspection and related investigation services for reported moisture, temperature anomalies and potential water leakage concerns in buildings. Service availability is subject to location, access, site conditions and confirmation by our team.

2. Enquiries and booking

A website enquiry or WhatsApp message is a request for contact, not a confirmed appointment or accepted service order. An inspection is booked only after the scope, location, date, applicable charges and other relevant details have been discussed and confirmed by both parties.

3. Inspection limitations

Thermal cameras measure surface temperature patterns; they do not see or directly measure water. Temperature patterns can be influenced by sunlight, weather, airflow, building materials, insulation, plumbing operation and other factors. A thermal anomaly is not, by itself, proof of a water leak or its exact source.

Additional moisture measurements, visual checks, plumbing tests or other investigations may be needed. Some conditions may not be detectable during a particular visit. No guarantee of finding every leak, identifying every cause or achieving a particular outcome is made.

  • Inform us of known hazards, restricted areas and relevant site conditions.
  • Obtain any permissions needed to access the property.
  • Keep children, pets and other occupants away from inspection equipment and work areas where appropriate.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

INSPECTION DISCLAIMER.

Please understand the capabilities and limitations of thermal imaging before relying on observations or arranging repair work.

Thermal imaging is a screening and investigation aid

Infrared cameras measure surface temperatures and display temperature differences. They do not see through walls, detect water directly or automatically identify the cause of a temperature anomaly.

Results depend on conditions

Sun exposure, ambient temperature, wind, rain, humidity, airflow, surface finish, insulation, building materials, plumbing use and other site conditions can affect thermal patterns and interpretation.

Further checks may be necessary

Thermal observations may indicate areas that deserve closer attention. Moisture meters, visual inspection, plumbing tests or other specialist assessments may be needed to confirm the presence, source or extent of a problem.

No promise of guaranteed detection

We do not claim 100% accuracy, guaranteed leak detection or that every hidden defect will be identified. A lack of an unusual thermal pattern does not prove that no leak or moisture problem exists.

Illustrative website images

Thermal graphics and equipment illustrations on this website are demonstrations only unless explicitly identified as actual inspection material. They are not evidence of a customer's property condition and should not be used to diagnose a building.

Not a substitute for specialist advice

Website information and inspection observations are not a substitute for structural engineering advice, a licensed plumbing assessment, electrical safety advice or other regulated professional services where those are required.

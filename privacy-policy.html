PRIVACY & DATA

PRIVACY POLICY.

This policy explains how Leakage Specialist handles information when you visit this website or contact us about thermal scanning, moisture investigation or leakage inspection services.

Important: The website currently uses a WhatsApp hand-off for inspection requests. It does not contain a website-side database form or automatic photo-upload service.

1. Who we are

This website is operated under the name Leakage Specialist, based in Nanded City, Pune, Maharashtra, India. For privacy questions, contact pawarbuildkon86@gmail.com or call +91 88081 88105.

2. Information you may provide

If you choose to contact us, you may provide your name, mobile number, email address, property location, inspection type, description of the issue, and information or images you choose to share with us. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other information that is not needed for an inspection enquiry.

3. How the website enquiry form works

When you submit the enquiry form, your browser prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. The message is not sent automatically: you review it and choose whether to press Send. The website does not itself store the form details in a website database. If you send the message, WhatsApp and its relevant service providers process it under their own terms and privacy information.

If you select a photo in the form, the website does not upload it. You must attach and send the photo yourself in WhatsApp if you wish to share it.

4. Why we use your information

Information you send directly to us may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand the reported property issue, discuss inspection suitability, arrange a visit, provide service-related communication and maintain appropriate business records.

5. Website hosting and third-party services

This website is designed for static hosting through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information as part of delivering and securing the website. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to external services such as WhatsApp, email and Google Maps. When you use those services, their own privacy policies and practices apply. We do not control third-party websites.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current website code does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics tracker. Your browser, hosting provider or third-party services may process technical data or use technologies under their own policies. If analytics, advertising, embedded maps or other tracking tools are added later, this policy should be updated before or when those tools are enabled.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not offer enquiry information for sale. We may share information when needed to respond to your request, with service providers you choose to use (such as WhatsApp or email), or where disclosure is required by applicable law. Do not share information through a third-party service unless you are comfortable with that service's terms.

8. Retention and security

Because the current website form does not store submissions on this website, information retention depends on the communication channel you use and the business records we need to maintain. We take reasonable care with information received directly, but no website, email or messaging service can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit an enquiry or may contact us to ask about information you have shared with us. We will consider reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law and any legitimate record-keeping obligations. Contact pawarbuildkon86@gmail.com.

10. Children

This website and its services are intended for people seeking property inspection services. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, services or applicable requirements change. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.
